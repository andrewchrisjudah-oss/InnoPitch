# KNOMO — Investor Pitch

## One-line pitch

KNOMO turns a university syllabus into a social learning feed: short videos, memes, and progress moments that make studying feel as natural as scrolling.

## The problem

University students are surrounded by educational content, but it is fragmented across lecture slides, chat groups, long videos, and generic social feeds. Existing learning platforms are often optimized for completion and administration, while social platforms optimize for attention without academic outcomes. Students need a faster way to discover relevant explanations, stay motivated, and see measurable progress.

## The solution

KNOMO combines the familiarity of a reels-based social product with the structure of a real syllabus.

- Personalized feeds organized around a student’s university interests and units
- Short educational videos, memes, and lessons matched to syllabus topics
- Featured reels with automatic playback for an engaging first moment
- Follow, like, save, search, and creator discovery flows
- Study achievements shown on profile and story-style creator avatars
- Study-hour tracking and progress surfaces that turn effort into social proof
- Video upload flow for student and educator creators
- Account creation, login, password recovery, and interest onboarding

## Why now

Short-form video has become a default learning and discovery behavior. At the same time, universities and independent educators need better ways to package course-aligned content for students who do not learn effectively from static documents alone. KNOMO sits at the intersection of creator tools, personalized learning, and social accountability.

## Product advantage

KNOMO is not a generic video library with a search box. The syllabus is the organizing layer. That gives every interaction context: a reel belongs to a course unit, a creator can build credibility around a subject, and a learner’s activity can become a visible achievement rather than an invisible timer.

## Initial customer wedge

Start with university students in technical and science-heavy courses where concepts are dense, revision is frequent, and peer explanation has high value. Seed the feed with student creators, teaching assistants, and faculty partners in a small number of departments. Expand course-by-course through campus ambassadors and creator referrals.

## Business model

1. **Freemium learner product:** free syllabus feed and core social features; paid tier for advanced study analytics, adaptive revision paths, and premium creator collections.
2. **Creator and educator tools:** optional subscriptions for upload workflows, syllabus mapping, analytics, and moderation controls.
3. **University partnerships:** department or institution plans for private course communities, verified content, and aggregate learning insights with privacy safeguards.

## Go-to-market

- Recruit a focused group of campus ambassadors in 3–5 departments.
- Launch creator challenges around difficult syllabus units and exam revision.
- Partner with teaching assistants and student societies to seed high-trust content.
- Measure activation by first interest selection, first reel completion, first save, and first follow.
- Use the best-performing syllabus units to expand into adjacent courses and campuses.

## Current prototype

The working KNOMO prototype already demonstrates:

- React, TypeScript, Tailwind, and Shadcn-style UI components
- FastAPI backend with SQLite persistence
- Matching MySQL schema for a production database path
- Authentication flows and interest onboarding
- Auto-playing featured video reel
- Upload, follow, save, like, profile, progress, and search interactions
- Syllabus-linked sample content and KNOMO seal branding

## Key metrics to prove next

- Day-1 and Day-7 learner retention
- Reels completed per active learner
- Save rate and follow conversion per reel
- Weekly study hours recorded
- Creator upload-to-view conversion
- Percentage of feed items mapped to a learner’s selected syllabus interests
- Campus ambassador and educator acquisition cost

## Roadmap

**Phase 1 — Validate engagement:** instrument events, recruit pilot students, improve content moderation, and test syllabus personalization.

**Phase 2 — Strengthen learning outcomes:** add spaced revision, knowledge checks inside reels, creator analytics, and verified educator profiles.

**Phase 3 — Scale distribution:** launch university workspaces, expand the content marketplace, and support more degree programs and regions.

## Investment opportunity

KNOMO is building the social layer for syllabus-aligned learning. The next investment would fund pilot campuses, creator/content operations, analytics, moderation, and production-grade infrastructure. The immediate goal is to prove that syllabus relevance plus social motivation can create stronger study habits than either traditional courseware or entertainment feeds alone.

## Closing

Students already scroll. KNOMO gives that behavior a better destination: content that is relevant to their degree, created by people they trust, and connected to progress they can actually see.
